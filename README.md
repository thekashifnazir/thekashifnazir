I'm a Senior Technical Architect at [Cloudhouse](https://www.cloudhouse.com), leading multi-cloud modernisation across AWS, Azure, GCP, and on-premises environments. I establish automation and application packaging practices that reduce risk and improve consistency, and partner with product and engineering teams across multiple Cloudhouse products to align architecture with business goals.

I apply practical AI to improve reliability and speed in delivery and operations, and I'm now building in public, documenting my learning journey through this GitHub and my [personal site](https://kashifnazir.com).

A major interest at the moment is verification and knowing whether a model's output is actually right, then building the harness that shows it. Code is AI-generated under my direction. The architecture, the guardrails and the testing discipline are mine.
